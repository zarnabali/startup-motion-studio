# Phase 2 — Domain & competitor research

Goal: make the video industry-standard *for its domain* and differentiated from competitors.

## 1. Identify the domain of the reference

What kind of video is it? (startup feature launch · app promo · brand film · UGC ad · founder story ·
explainer · App Store preview · data/stat reel · lookbook). Who made it and for which category?
The domain decides the benchmarks — a SaaS feature launch and a consumer brand film follow different norms.

## 2. Research (WebSearch + WebFetch; record URLs)

1. **Domain standards** — 3–5 best-in-class examples of this video type from the last 12 months
   (e.g. Linear / Arc / Notion / Apple launch films for product launches; the category leaders for
   consumer brands). For each: length, aspect, pacing, hook, type, motion, music, CTA.
2. **Competitors on the same message** — refresh `brand/COMPETITORS.md` rows for primary
   (direct competitors), secondary (adjacent products) and tertiary (what
   users do instead today) players: how do they show *this* feature? What claims,
   visuals and clichés do they use? Check their Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/App Store previews where reachable.
3. **Platform now** — current format/length/caption norms for the target channels (see `platform-specs.md`;
   refresh if older than 3 months).
4. **Mood references** — Pinterest / Behance / Dribbble / SSENSE editorials for look & feel. Pinterest
   API public search is closed-beta and scraping breaks its ToS, so: browse, or ask the owner to save a
   board and drop images into `projects/<job>/02-research/mood/`. **Mood images are never used as assets.**

## 3. Write `02-research/RESEARCH.md`

- Domain benchmarks table · competitor treatment of this message · what's overused (avoid) · what's open
  (own) · platform constraints · 5–10 sourced insights.
- Append dated findings to the "Job log" of `brand/COMPETITORS.md`.

## 4. Pitch three concepts

Each = **Reference DNA × one brand strength (BRAND_BRAIN §4) × one open gap (COMPETITORS "Gaps")**:

```
Concept A — "<title>"
One line: …
Hook (0–1.5 s): …
Arc: hook → … → CTA
Why it beats competitors: …
Reference fidelity: structural / loose / shot-for-shot
Risk: …
```

Recommend one with a reason. Gate = the owner picks.
